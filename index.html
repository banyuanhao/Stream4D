Stream4D: 4D-Consistency Reinforcement Learning for Streaming Autoregressive Video Models

A lightweight RL recipe that rewards coherent moving worlds rather than frozen, rigid scenes.

Abstract

Streaming autoregressive diffusion models generate videos chunk by chunk, enabling real-time generation over long horizons. However, local frame objectives do not sufficiently constrain the geometry and dynamics of a coherent world. Existing static-3D reconstruction rewards can make this worse: a single rigid reconstruction cannot model dynamic objects, so motion is treated as reconstruction error and the policy can earn reward by freezing the scene.

Stream4D replaces the rigid critic with a feed-forward 4D Gaussian-Splatting reconstructor and adds a target-centered motion prior plus a lightweight perceptual anchor. The result is a transferable reward that improves 4D consistency while preserving natural motion across multiple streaming AR backbones.

Key idea. Reward dynamic scenes with a dynamic 4D representation. Then add a peaked motion gate so the policy cannot win by freezing or by producing jittery, incoherent motion.

How Stream4D works

4D reconstruction consistency

MoVieS reconstructs the rollout as a dynamic 4D Gaussian scene and re-renders it at estimated cameras. High reward means the video can be explained as a coherent evolving scene.

Natural-motion prior

A peaked Gaussian target on scene-flow magnitude encourages enough motion, but not runaway blur. Smoothness and rigidity terms penalize jitter and spatially erratic flow.

Perceptual anchor

HPSv2 stabilizes appearance so the policy does not trade visual quality for geometry and motion reward. The three axes are z-normalized and summed.
